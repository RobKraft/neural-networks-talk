Things to notice

  • Reset with "Shuffled" order a few times. The step count and final weights change every run. Many different weight sets give the same right answers, so training isn't deterministic.
  • The model is only the table above. Once training stops, you can ship those numbers as a finished, frozen model.
  • Try to "debug" a weight. Why is one cell −3? No single number explains a decision. That's why nobody can just patch a model's bad answer.
  • Light up a non-digit shape. The network still answers confidently. It can only choose from what it was trained on.
  • Someone has to know the right answers. Every update needs a label, which makes this supervised learning.
